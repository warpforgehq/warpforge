Keep the tasks you are juggling one click away. Every task you open now gets a tab in the window's title bar, beside the window buttons. Closing a tab never stops or archives the task — it just leaves the strip. Drag tabs to reorder them, middle-click to close, press ⌃Tab to cycle, and ⌘⇧T to reopen the last one you closed.

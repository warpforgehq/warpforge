**Team** brings your parallel sessions into one conversation. Open it from the sidebar to read what your sessions tell each other — per project, with each project's channel and every direct exchange, whether a message was delivered, is waiting, or was refused and why. Post to a project's channel or message a single session from there, and type `@` to hand a post straight to the sessions you name: they pick it up as your instruction right away. **Start a session** turns any of your posts into a new task with the agent and model you choose. When two sessions message each other too much, a "needs you" item tells you, and you can mute a session or pause a whole project at any time. Each session's chat also shows the messages it sent and received, with a link to the other task.

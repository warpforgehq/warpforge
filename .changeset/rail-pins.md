Get straight to work from the New Task composer. Your running and blocked tasks are visible in the sidebar, and you can pin a task to the left rail to reopen it with one click. Existing pinned tasks move to the rail with their agent and status. You can also pin projects, Smiths and channels, drag pins to reorder them, and press ⌥⌘4 to open Automations.

**Tasks and chats each get their own list.** Click the sidebar title to switch between **Tasks**, your project work, and **Chats**, your conversations without a project, or to jump into any space or project. Opening a chat takes you to Chats, and a dot on the title tells you when a chat in the other list needs you. The same menu sits on every project and space, so Tasks and Chats are always one click away, and clicking **Tasks** on the rail takes you back to the list.

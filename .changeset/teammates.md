**Teammates** are agents with a name that stay with you across tasks. Create one in **Team → Teammates**, give it a voice and rules, the projects it may work in, a model, daily limits and what it may push or open, and it remembers what it decided and tried from one task to the next. Hand it work by typing `@Name` in a project channel or in its own conversation — it answers, files a board item, or starts the work and reports back when it is done — or with **Start work**, which opens a normal task on the board run by that teammate. Assign local board items to a teammate and see them by name on the board. You can pause or delete a teammate at any time, and if its soul or charter is edited outside Warpforge it waits for you to keep or undo the change before starting anything.

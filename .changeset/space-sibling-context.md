Group the repositories one product lives in into a space, and agents on new tasks can see all of them. Create a space in Settings → Spaces & repos, or right-click a project and choose Add to space…; a repo can also list its siblings under `linked:` in its workspace.yaml. Open a space from the project switcher or pin it to the rail to see its projects and folders and the tasks of all of them in one place. A task in a space shows +N repos in the title row, so you always know which folders its agent can see.

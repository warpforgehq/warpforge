Agents can now answer with visuals. Ask for a chart, a table, a diagram or a clickable mockup and it appears right in the task's chat, above the agent's reply, in your theme in light and dark. Pages run sandboxed, away from your files and sessions, and their links open in the in-app browser. On macOS, agents can preview a page in the desktop app before showing it.

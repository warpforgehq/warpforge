Find your way around faster with an always-there rail. New task, Tasks, Inbox, Smiths and Automations now sit in a slim rail on the left edge that never goes away, with badges for unread pull requests and Smiths messages. The panel beside it shows whatever you picked, and ⌘\ folds it away when you want the room, leaving the rail in place. Settings now open right there too: the panel switches to the settings sections and the page fills the main area, so there is no extra screen to close — press Esc or pick anything in the rail to get back.

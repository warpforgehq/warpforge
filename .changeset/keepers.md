Keepers review every commit before you do. Open a task that runs in its own worktree, choose the new **Keepers** tab at the end of the rail and pick **Add keeper** — Review for bugs and edge cases, Slop for code that reads as generated, or Conventions for your project's written rules. Each keeper checks what the agent commits and hands its findings back to the same agent as one fix request, shown in the chat as **Keepers asked for a fix**. The tab shows every keeper's last ten runs at a glance, lists open findings with a link to the exact line, and lets you **Dismiss** the ones that are wrong so they are not raised again. When fixes stall, the keeper stops and asks you to extend, accept the change as it is, or switch it off for the task. The Keepers button and the task's row in the sidebar turn red when a keeper needs you, and switching a keeper off for one task leaves the rest untouched.

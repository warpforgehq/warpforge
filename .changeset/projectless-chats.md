Start a chat without a project. Pick **No project** in the project picker on New task, and the agent works in a folder of its own: good for questions, drafts and experiments that do not belong to a codebase yet. Chats sit in their own **Chats** group at the top of the sidebar, above your projects, with the Explorer and a Terminal on that folder and **Open folder** to see it in Finder. When the idea turns into real work, **Continue in project…** carries the conversation into a new task in the project you pick, and the chat stays available. Deleting a chat asks whether its files go too.

Dictate to your agents instead of typing. Press ⌘⇧D, or click the microphone in the chat composer, and speak in Ukrainian, Russian, English or dozens of other languages — even mixed in one sentence. The text lands at your cursor for you to review before sending; hold ⌘⇧D for push-to-talk, and press Esc to discard. Transcription runs entirely on your Mac, so your voice never leaves it. Add custom words in Settings → Voice to keep names and technical terms spelled right.

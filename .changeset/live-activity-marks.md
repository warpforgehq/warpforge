See what's still running at a glance. A task's row in the sidebar shows a small terminal mark while one of its terminals is open, and each project's row shows a mark while its services or port-forwards run, turning red when one has failed. Inside a task, the Runtime and Terminal buttons on the rail carry a green dot while something there runs, or a red one when something failed; point at a task's mark or a rail button to see exactly what. The Terminal count now shows only the task's own terminals.
